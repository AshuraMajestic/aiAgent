NIMBUS DYNAMICS PVT. LTD. - TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
Document ID: FIN-TRV-007 | Version 2.8 | Effective: 1 April 2026 | Owner: Finance Department

1. SCOPE
This policy covers business travel and reimbursable expenses for all employees. Travel must be pre-approved by the reporting manager in the "TravelDesk" module of PeopleHub.

2. TRAVEL APPROVAL
- Domestic travel: manager approval, at least 5 working days in advance.
- International travel: manager + Department Head + Finance approval, at least 15 working days in advance.
- Travel booking must be done through the company travel partner, "WingsAway Travels".

3. AIR TRAVEL
- Domestic flights: economy class only. Business class is allowed for L5 and above (VP level).
- International flights: economy class for flights under 8 hours; premium economy for flights over 8 hours for L4 and above.
- Book at least 7 days in advance to get the lowest fare. Last-minute bookings above INR 15,000 for domestic flights need Department Head approval.

4. TRAIN AND ROAD TRAVEL
- Train: AC 2-tier (2A) for L1-L3; AC 1st class for L4 and above.
- Local taxi / cab (Uber, Ola): reimbursable for client visits and airport transfers. Use of auto-rickshaw is reimbursable with a receipt.
- Personal car: INR 12 per km; personal two-wheeler: INR 5 per km. A log sheet is required.

5. HOTEL ACCOMMODATION (PER NIGHT, INCLUDING TAXES)
  Tier-1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata):
    L1-L2: INR 4,500 | L3: INR 6,000 | L4 and above: INR 8,500
  Other cities:
    L1-L2: INR 3,000 | L3: INR 4,000 | L4 and above: INR 5,500
  International: USD 150 per night for L1-L3; USD 220 for L4 and above.
Hotels should be booked through WingsAway. Stays beyond 10 days require use of serviced apartments.

6. DAILY ALLOWANCE (MEALS & INCIDENTALS)
  Domestic: INR 1,000 per day (L1-L3); INR 1,500 per day (L4 and above)
  International: USD 60 per day (L1-L3); USD 90 per day (L4 and above)
No meal allowance is paid if meals are provided by the client or at a conference. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

7. OTHER REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
- Internet / mobile data during travel: up to INR 500 per trip.
- Visa fees and mandatory travel insurance for international trips.
- Client entertainment: up to INR 2,500 per person, with prior approval and the client's name noted on the bill.
- Conference fees if approved by the manager under the learning budget.

8. NON-REIMBURSABLE EXPENSES
Traffic fines, personal shopping, mini-bar charges, spa or entertainment, extra baggage for personal items, and lost or stolen personal belongings.

9. CLAIM SUBMISSION
- Submit expenses in the "ExpenseHub" module within 10 working days of returning from the trip.
- Attach original receipts for every expense above INR 500.
- Manager approves within 3 working days, Finance verifies within 5 working days.
- Payment is credited with the next salary or within 10 working days, whichever is earlier.
- Claims submitted after 30 days will be rejected unless the Finance Controller approves an exception.

10. ADVANCES
A travel advance of up to 70% of the estimated trip cost can be requested. It must be settled within 7 days of returning.
