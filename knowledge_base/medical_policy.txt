NIMBUS DYNAMICS PVT. LTD. - MEDICAL & HEALTH BENEFITS POLICY
Document ID: HR-MED-004 | Version 3.0 | Policy year: 1 April 2026 - 31 March 2027 | Owner: Benefits Team

1. GROUP HEALTH INSURANCE
Nimbus Dynamics provides group health insurance through "CarePlus Insurance" (policy number NDX-GHI-2026-0187). Coverage starts from the employee's date of joining.

2. COVERAGE AMOUNTS (PER YEAR)
  Employee + spouse + up to 2 children: INR 5,00,000 floater cover
  Parents / parents-in-law (optional): INR 3,00,000 cover, premium paid by the employee (about INR 9,500 per year)
  Senior employees (L4 and above): INR 8,00,000 floater cover

3. WHAT IS COVERED
- Hospitalization of 24 hours or more
- Pre-hospitalization (30 days) and post-hospitalization (60 days) expenses
- Day-care procedures such as cataract surgery and dialysis
- Maternity: normal delivery up to INR 50,000; C-section up to INR 80,000
- New-born baby cover from day 1
- Ambulance charges up to INR 3,000 per claim
- Pre-existing diseases are covered after a 12-month waiting period

4. WHAT IS NOT COVERED
- Cosmetic or plastic surgery (unless needed after an accident)
- Dental treatment, except in case of accidents
- Spectacles and contact lenses
- Self-inflicted injuries and injuries under the influence of alcohol or drugs
- Experimental or unproven treatments

5. CASHLESS CLAIM PROCESS
1. Visit a network hospital (over 4,500 hospitals are listed on the CarePlus app).
2. Show the e-health card and a government photo ID at the TPA desk.
3. The hospital sends a pre-authorization request; approval usually takes 4-6 hours.
4. The insurer settles the bill directly with the hospital. The employee pays only non-covered items.

6. REIMBURSEMENT CLAIM PROCESS
If treatment is taken at a non-network hospital, pay the bill and submit these documents within 30 days of discharge: claim form, original bills, discharge summary, investigation reports, and a cancelled cheque. Reimbursement is processed within 21 working days.

7. OTHER WELLNESS BENEFITS
- Annual health check-up: free for every employee once a year at partner labs (HealthFirst Diagnostics). Employees aged 40+ get an extended panel.
- Mental health support: 6 free counselling sessions per year with certified counsellors through the "MindEase" helpline: 1800-555-0177 (24x7, confidential).
- Gym / fitness reimbursement: up to INR 6,000 per year with proof of payment.
- Eye check-up camp is held every October at each office.
