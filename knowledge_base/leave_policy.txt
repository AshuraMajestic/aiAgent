NIMBUS DYNAMICS PVT. LTD. - LEAVE POLICY
Document ID: HR-LV-003 | Version 2.5 | Effective: 1 January 2026 | Owner: Human Resources

1. LEAVE YEAR
The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. All leave is applied for and approved through the PeopleHub portal.

2. TYPES OF LEAVE

2.1 Casual Leave (CL): 8 days per year. Cannot be carried forward. A maximum of 3 consecutive days may be taken at a time without special approval.

2.2 Earned / Privilege Leave (EL): 18 days per year, credited at 1.5 days per month. Up to 30 days can be carried forward. Encashment is allowed for balances above 15 days at the time of exit or at year end (maximum 10 days encashed per year).

2.3 Sick Leave (SL): 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave lapses at year end.

2.4 Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children, and 12 weeks for the third child onwards, as per the Maternity Benefit Act. An additional 6 weeks of work-from-home is available on request.

2.5 Paternity Leave: 10 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.

2.6 Bereavement Leave: 5 days of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member (parent, spouse, child, sibling).

2.7 Marriage Leave: 5 days of paid leave, once during employment, for the employee's own marriage.

2.8 Compensatory Off: Employees who work on a weekend or public holiday with manager approval get a comp-off, which must be used within 60 days.

2.9 Loss of Pay (LOP): Leave taken without balance is treated as LOP, and salary is deducted on a per-day basis (monthly salary / 30).

3. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
The company observes 12 public holidays per year, published each December. Employees also get 2 optional (floating) holidays per year chosen from a list of 6.

4. APPLYING FOR LEAVE
- Planned leave of 3 days or more should be applied at least 7 days in advance.
- Emergency leave must be informed to the manager by 10:30 AM on the same day, and applied in the portal within 2 working days.
- Managers must approve or reject requests within 2 working days; otherwise the request is escalated to the HR Business Partner.

5. LEAVE DURING PROBATION
Employees on probation accrue leave normally but may use a maximum of 1 EL day per month. Maternity and paternity leave are available only after 80 days of service.

6. SANDWICH RULE
Weekends or holidays falling between two leave days are counted as leave only if the employee takes more than 5 consecutive leave days.
