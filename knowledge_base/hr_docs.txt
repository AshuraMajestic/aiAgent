NIMBUS DYNAMICS PVT. LTD. - HR POLICY HANDBOOK
Document ID: HR-POL-002 | Version 4.0 | Effective: 1 April 2026 | Owner: Human Resources

1. ABOUT THE COMPANY
Nimbus Dynamics Pvt. Ltd. is a cloud software company founded in 2015 by Rohan Mehta (CEO) and Anjali Rao (CTO). It builds logistics-tracking and fleet-analytics software. Headquarters: Bengaluru. Other offices: Hyderabad and Pune. Total employees: approximately 420.

2. WORKING HOURS
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, during which employees must be available. Saturdays and Sundays are weekly offs.

3. WORK-FROM-HOME (HYBRID MODEL)
Employees follow a hybrid model: 3 days in office (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) and 2 days remote. Managers may approve extra WFH days for up to 10 days per year for personal emergencies. Fully remote roles require VP approval.

4. DRESS CODE
Business casual from Tuesday to Thursday. Casual wear on Monday and Friday. Formal wear is expected during client visits.

5. PROBATION
All new employees serve a 6-month probation. A review is conducted at 3 months and again at 6 months. Confirmation is communicated through a formal letter from HR.

6. PERFORMANCE REVIEW
Performance cycles run twice a year: April-September (mid-year review in October) and October-March (annual review in April). Ratings use a 5-point scale: 1 = Needs Improvement, 2 = Partially Meets, 3 = Meets, 4 = Exceeds, 5 = Outstanding. Annual increments range from 0% to 18% depending on rating.

7. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees must treat colleagues with respect, avoid conflicts of interest, protect confidential data, and not accept gifts worth more than INR 2,000 from vendors or clients. Harassment of any kind is a zero-tolerance offence.

8. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
Employees can raise concerns with their manager, the HR Business Partner, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline: ethics@nimbusdynamics.example or 1800-555-0142. The Internal Committee (IC) handles harassment complaints and acts within 30 days.

9. EXIT PROCESS
After resignation, the employee completes handover, returns assets (laptop, ID card, access tokens), attends an exit interview, and receives the full and final settlement within 45 days of the last working day. Experience and relieving letters are issued within 7 working days after settlement.
