NIMBUS DYNAMICS PVT. LTD. - HIRING PROCESS
Document ID: HR-REC-001 | Version 3.2 | Last updated: 12 January 2026 | Owner: Talent Acquisition Team

1. PURPOSE
This document describes how Nimbus Dynamics hires employees, from raising a job requisition to the candidate's first day. It applies to all full-time roles in India (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune).

2. HIRING STAGES
Stage 1 - Requisition: The hiring manager raises a requisition in the HRMS portal "PeopleHub". It must be approved by the Department Head and the Finance Controller. Approval takes up to 5 working days.

Stage 2 - Sourcing: The Talent Acquisition (TA) team posts the role on LinkedIn, Naukri and the company careers page (careers.nimbusdynamics.example). Employee referrals are open for 14 days before external posting for senior roles.

Stage 3 - Resume Screening: TA screens resumes within 3 working days. Shortlisted candidates receive a call from a recruiter.

Stage 4 - Interview Rounds:
  Round 1 - Online coding or aptitude assessment (60 minutes) on the HackerHub platform. Passing score: 65%.
  Round 2 - Technical interview (45-60 minutes) with two engineers.
  Round 3 - Managerial interview (30-45 minutes) with the hiring manager.
  Round 4 - HR interview (30 minutes) covering culture fit, salary expectations and notice period.
  Freshers (campus hires) skip Round 3.

Stage 5 - Offer: The offer letter is issued within 4 working days of the final interview. The candidate has 7 days to accept. Offers are signed digitally through DocuSign.

Stage 6 - Background Verification (BGV): Conducted by an external agency, TrustCheck. It covers education, last two employers and criminal record. BGV usually takes 7-10 working days.

Stage 7 - Onboarding: Day 1 is always a Monday (or the next working day). The new hire receives a laptop, ID card and a buddy.

3. SALARY BANDS (ANNUAL CTC, INDIA)
  Associate Engineer (L1): INR 6,00,000 - 9,00,000
  Software Engineer (L2): INR 9,00,000 - 16,00,000
  Senior Engineer (L3): INR 16,00,000 - 28,00,000
  Lead Engineer (L4): INR 28,00,000 - 42,00,000

4. EMPLOYEE REFERRAL BONUS
  L1-L2 referral: INR 20,000
  L3 referral: INR 35,000
  L4 and above: INR 50,000
The bonus is paid after the referred employee completes 90 days. Referrals for HR or Finance roles by employees of the same team are not eligible.

5. NOTICE PERIOD
Standard notice period is 60 days for L1-L3 and 90 days for L4 and above. Buyout is possible with manager and HR approval.
